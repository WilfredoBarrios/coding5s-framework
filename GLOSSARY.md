\# 📖 GLOSSARY.md: Coding5s Technical Lexicon



This glossary defines the technical, pedagogical, and architectural terminology that constitutes the Coding5s ecosystem. Terms marked with (\*) are concepts coined specifically for this framework.



\---



\### A

\* \*\*Architectural Ledger (\*):\*\* A persistent data store within \*Stateful5s\* that tracks a student’s technical decisions, architectural preferences, and mastered concepts across the entire curriculum.

\* \*\*Architecture-as-Code (\*):\*\* The practice of treating pedagogical curricula as a version-controlled data pipeline, where logical updates automatically propagate through all localized/translated versions.

\* \*\*Audit-First Approach (\*):\*\* A pedagogical paradigm where the primary competency is the ability to analyze, critique, and refactor existing logic rather than drafting new code.



\### B

\* \*\*Brownfield Triage (\*):\*\* A diagnostic technique where students are forced to work within "broken" or sub-optimal codebases (AST errors, lexer failures) to identify the root cause of failure.



\### C

\* \*\*Coding5s Methodology (\*):\*\* A 5-stage learning system (Practice, Debug, Complete, Refactor, Extend) designed to mirror professional software development lifecycles.

\* \*\*Cognitive Outsourcing:\*\* The act of delegating critical thinking and logic derivation to an unconstrained LLM, resulting in a loss of deep-skill acquisition.

\* \*\*Composition Priority (\*):\*\* An immutable hierarchy rule within the \*ECP\* that dictates which pedagogical or stylistic constraints override others during multi-mentor context stacking.

\* \*\*Controlled Cognitive Friction (\*):\*\* The intentional design of pedagogical obstacles that force the brain to engage in high-effort retrieval practice, preventing passive consumption.



\### E

\* \*\*ECP (Ephemeral Context Protocol) (\*):\*\* An orchestration layer that synchronizes modular "context chips" (Seed Contexts) to maintain AI alignment across different stages of the learning lifecycle.

\* \*\*Epistemic Debt (\*):\*\* The accumulated deficit of fundamental understanding caused by relying on AI-generated solutions without comprehending the underlying logic.

\* \*\*Epistemic Sovereignty (\*):\*\* The learner’s ultimate authority to validate and audit AI outputs; treating the LLM as a tool for verification rather than a source of truth.

\* \*\*Extend (\*):\*\* Stage 5 of the Coding5s lifecycle, focusing on the creative scaling, integration, and authorship of systems.



\### G

\* \*\*General Seed Context (\*):\*\* A "Meta-Prompt" used to generate specialized \*Seed Contexts\*. It acts as the universal law governing the curvature of an AI’s latent space.

\* \*\*Greenfield Creation:\*\* The act of building software from scratch. Coding5s treats this as a secondary skill compared to auditing and refactoring.



\### H

\* \*\*Hive Mind Mentors (\*):\*\* The swarm of specialized AI nodes (Socratic, Translator, Legacy Archaeologist) that collaborate to guide a student through a unified epistemic state.



\### L

\* \*\*Latent Drift:\*\* The tendency of LLMs to regress toward generic statistical means and lose their specialized persona or constraint adherence during long interactions.

\* \*\*Localization Sandbox (\*):\*\* An automated CI/CD pipeline used to test localized prompts against specific linguistic constraints before deployment.

\* \*\*Low-Resource Empowerment (\*):\*\* The capability of the \*Seed Context\* pipeline to enable high-tier technical education in languages with minimal training data.



\### M

\* \*\*Meta-Prompt Foundry (\*):\*\* The Level 0 architectural component that generates specialized \*Seed Factories\* for any given cognitive dimension.



\### P

\* \*\*Post-AI Era Readiness (\*):\*\* The pedagogical focus on high-level system architecture, logic auditing, and debugging rather than rote syntactic memorization.



\### S

\* \*\*Seed Context (\*):\*\* A high-density (150-250 word) prompt injection that constrains an AI's latent space to prevent epistemic debt and ensure pedagogical rigor.

\* \*\*Semantic Isolation (\*):\*\* A technique used in the \*Complete\* stage where students must fill in logic gaps whose inputs are known but whose internal state transformations are hidden.

\* \*\*Socratic Concept Guide (\*):\*\* A specialized mentor archetype forbidden from direct lecturing, mandated to use analogies and guided inquiry to trigger student deduction.

\* \*\*State Validation Gate (\*):\*\* A deterministic checkpoint (e.g., automated testing) that ensures a student’s state is robust before allowing progression to the next stage.

\* \*\*Stateful5s (\*):\*\* The cumulative state management engine that tracks the student’s learning path, preventing them from proceeding if foundational logic is flawed.



\### T

\* \*\*Translator Mentors (\*):\*\* Specialized agents that map computational logic onto native conceptual frameworks to bypass colonial translation paradigms.
