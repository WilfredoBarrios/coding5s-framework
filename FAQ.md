\# ❓ FAQ: Frequently Asked Questions



Direct answers on how Coding5s transforms technical education in the era of AI.



\---



\## 🎓 For Teachers



Direct answers on how Coding5s changes your classroom dynamics.



\### The Learning Problem



\*\*Q: My students no longer think, they just outsource everything to AI. Does this help?\*\*

A: Yes, and it attacks the problem right at its root. In Coding5s, the first stage (Practice) forces the student to manually copy the reference code into their own editor before interacting with any AI. This physical act fixes muscle memory and syntax in the brain, establishing a real foundation before the AI enters the picture. Only afterwards, when the student uses the specialized Mentor prompts, does the Socratic mode kick in: the AI does not give them the answer; it asks difficult questions so they can reach the solution themselves. It is a shift from "copying and pasting from AI" to "building with your own hands."



\*\*Q: Is it very difficult for them to learn?\*\*

A: Quite the opposite. The student only has to follow the 5 stages. It is a structure that gives them confidence: they know exactly what they need to do (first they practice, then they correct, then they complete, after that they optimize, and finally they extend). It eliminates the paralysis of "where do I start?".



\*\*Q: How do I evaluate 40 students with this system without losing my mind?\*\*

A: The Student Kit includes a self-evaluation grid where each student marks the lessons they are studying: with \*\*1\*\* for those they master easily, with \*\*-1\*\* for those they struggled with, and with \*\*0\*\* for those they haven't touched yet. This gives you an instant visual map of the group's progress without having to correct every single line of code. An automated evaluation platform with more advanced metrics is on the roadmap, but for now, this simple and effective system allows you to do human tracking at a large scale.



\### Practical Implementation



\*\*Q: I am a developer, but I am not an AI expert. Do I need to know how to program complex prompts?\*\*

A: Not at all. You use an Excel template. If you know how to use Excel to input student names or grades, you already know how to create a course in Coding5s. Excel "writes" the prompts for you. You only define the topic and the difficulty level.



\*\*Q: How long will it take me to transition my current class into this format?\*\*

A: No more than a couple of hours. You just need to follow the course creation guide using the Creator Kit: you define the syllabus, Excel generates the prompts automatically, and at the end, you export a clean "Student Kit" file (no formulas, just the final prompts ready for your students to use). You don't have to throw away your current material; you just repackage it so it is "AI-proof."



\*\*Q: I already have a curriculum approved by my university. Do I have to change everything?\*\*

A: No. Coding5s works as a \*\*complement\*\* to what already exists: it is a methodological alternative that teaches through the 5 stages. It does not replace your approved curriculum, but it does enrich it. That being said, someone \*can\* learn to program using solely this method, because the 5 stages cover the entire learning cycle, from basic syntax to scalable architecture.



\*\*Q: How much does this cost? Can I use it with 500 students without spending a fortune?\*\*

A: Coding5s is open source (MIT license), so the framework is 100% free. The only cost is access to an LLM. You can use free models (ChatGPT free, Gemini, Claude free) and you will get very good results for the early stages. For the final stages (especially Stage 5, where prompts are more complex and require deep reasoning), a premium model is recommended. But with 500 students, you do not need to pay per-student license fees.



\*\*Q: Do I have to install anything strange in my school's computer labs?\*\*

A: No. You only need three things: (1) Microsoft Excel to open the Creator Kit/Student Kit, (2) a free IDE (VS Code, PyCharm Community, etc.) for students to write code, and (3) access to any AI chat in the browser. No servers, databases, or special IT permissions required.



\### Control and Quality



\*\*Q: How do I know if the system is actually working?\*\*

A: Because you will see a shift in the type of questions they ask you. They will stop asking you "why isn't my code running?" and start asking you about design logic. You reclaim your role as a teacher, rather than being a syntax error finder.



\*\*Q: What if the AI gives away too big of a hint and "ruins" the student's effort?\*\*

A: Mentors operate under the "No Keyboard" rule (the AI cannot write code for the student). If the student asks for a direct answer, the Mentor activates a Socratic barrier. As a teacher, you can adjust the level of "friction" in Excel from "General Hints" all the way to "Ruthless Mode." You have full control over the difficulty.



\*\*Q: What happens if the AI goes haywire and starts talking nonsense?\*\*

A: The system has "safety guardrails" with very strict rules. But if it happens, as a teacher, you have full control within your Excel sheet: you adjust the instruction, regenerate the prompt, and that's it. You don't depend on anyone else to fix anything.



\### Special Cases



\*\*Q: I have students who speak Mayan languages (Kaqchikel, Q'eqchi'). Does this work for them?\*\*

A: Yes, and it is one of the unique strengths of Coding5s. The system features specialized "Language Seed Contexts" that allow teaching programming in low-resource languages without losing technical precision. The AI does not translate literally; it maps computational concepts to native conceptual frameworks.



\*\*Q: Is this only for programming?\*\*

A: We started with programming because that is where AI is currently "destroying" learning the most, but the 5-stage structure works for any subject where the student has to solve real-world problems. If you have a process-based subject (researching, testing, correcting, presenting), this will work for you, though it might require restructuring the rules that generate the prompts.



\### Community and Support



\*\*Q: What happens if I get stuck while configuring something?\*\*

A: Coding5s is open source and in its early stages. What we are looking for is to build a collaborative community of teachers and developers who improve the method together. If something doesn't work for you or you have an idea to improve it, open an "Issue" or "Discussion" on GitHub. Your contribution can become part of the standard we are creating together.

\## 🚀 For Students (Junior / Self-Taught)



Real questions from someone who wants to stop watching tutorials and start becoming a developer.



\### The "Tutorial Hell" Problem



\*\*Q: I feel like I understand the video when I watch it, but if I close the browser, I don't know how to write anything. Does Coding5s help with that?\*\*

A: Exactly. Coding5s eliminates the "spectator" mode. \*\*Stage 1 (Practice)\*\* forces you to manually copy code into your editor, fixing muscle memory before the AI even enters. Afterwards, you move on to stages like "Debug" or "Complete," where something is already built and you have to get your hands dirty. You learn by moving pieces with your own hands, not by watching others move them.



\*\*Q: Why is this system better than just asking ChatGPT everything?\*\*

A: Because ChatGPT gives you the answer and you forget it 5 minutes later. In Coding5s, you first build the foundation yourself in Stage 1 (without AI involved). When you already master the fundamentals and need help, you activate the Mentor prompts, which are forbidden from giving you direct solutions: they will ask you questions so that you \*deduce\* the logic. It's like having a teacher right next to you who won't let you cheat, but guides you so you are the one solving it.



\*\*Q: How is this different from a "Learn React in 10 Weeks" bootcamp?\*\*

A: Traditional bootcamps teach you a specific stack that might be obsolete in 2-5 years. Coding5s does not teach you a framework; it teaches you how to LEARN any framework quickly. By the time you finish, you will know how to audit a codebase, debug a problem, and refactor a component. That is what makes you employable in the long run.



\### About the Learning Process



\*\*Q: I am afraid of breaking things. What happens if I make a mistake?\*\*

A: That is exactly what we are looking for! The "Debug" stage is mandatory. You are not here for everything to go right on the first try; you are here to learn how to detect what failed. If the code breaks, the system helps you understand \*why\*, turning every mistake into a technical lesson instead of a frustration.



\*\*Q: I already know some Python, do I have to start at Stage 1 (Practice) like a baby?\*\*

A: No. The Student Kit has a grid where you mark the lessons you already master. You can start directly from any point that makes sense for your level. The AI will generate appropriate exercises based on where you choose to begin. Coding5s does not hold you back in the basics if you already master them; it throws you into the deep end.



\*\*Q: What happens if I get frustrated and want to quit? Is the system too harsh?\*\*

A: Yes, it is going to be hard, and that is the point. Frustration is a sign that you are learning. When you use the Mentor prompts, you can treat them like an expert friend in the language: tell them your doubt, tell them where you are stuck, and they will step into the role of an expert who will guide you step-by-step, telling you exactly how to handle each situation without gifting you the answer.



\### The Reality of the Job



\*\*Q: Why am I wasting time refactoring? I just want the code to work.\*\*

A: Because in a real job, making the code "work" is only 20% of the task. The other 80% is making sure the code is clean, maintainable, and won't give headaches to other developers. In Coding5s, the "Refactor" stage teaches you to write professional code, not just code that runs.



\*\*Q: Does this help me get a job? What do I put in my portfolio?\*\*

A: Yes. In Coding5s, you don't just write code; you document decisions, audit legacy code, and refactor systems. When you apply for jobs, you won't say "I did 50 tutorials"; you will say "I audited a codebase, identified vulnerabilities, and refactored the authentication module." That is what companies are looking for in 2026.



\*\*Q: Does this prepare me to work remotely for US companies?\*\*

A: Exactly. The market is moving toward "nearshore" talent. Coding5s trains you in the skills that US companies look for in Latin American talent: the ability to audit AI-generated code, communicate with stakeholders, and adapt quickly to new stacks.



\*\*Q: Does this prepare me for a real job or is it just theory?\*\*

A: It prepares you for a real job because it replicates a company's lifecycle: Development -> Testing -> Integration -> Maintenance -> Scaling. When you get to an interview, you will be able to explain how you audit code and how you repair flaws. That is what companies look for today.



\### What Do I Need?



\*\*Q: Do I need a super powerful computer or to install a thousand programs?\*\*

A: No. You only need three things: (1) the \*\*Student Kit\*\* (an Excel file with the prompts ready), (2) a \*\*free IDE\*\* that runs the language you are learning (VS Code, PyCharm Community, etc.), and (3) access to any \*\*AI chat\*\* in your browser. The methodology lives in your way of working, not in heavy tools.



\*\*Q: Do I have to pay for an expensive ChatGPT or Claude subscription to use this?\*\*

A: Not necessarily. Coding5s is model-agnostic. You can use free models and get very good results, especially in the early stages. For the final stages (especially Stage 5) where the prompts are more complex, a premium model is recommended, but it is not mandatory.



\*\*Q: Do I have to use specific tools? Does it work with VS Code, GitHub, etc.?\*\*

A: Coding5s is agnostic. It works with your favorite editor (VS Code, Vim, IntelliJ), your version control system (GitHub, GitLab), and your preferred LLM (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, or local models). We do not lock you into an ecosystem.



\*\*Q: What do I do if I get blocked and don't even know what to ask anymore?\*\*

A: You have the specialized Mentors. You are not alone in front of a generic chat. If you get blocked, you can consult the Concept Mentor to get an analogy, or the Legacy Code Mentor to help you read through a complex file. The system is designed so that you always have an exit door, but it always requires your own effort.

\## 🏢 For Companies and CTOs



Questions from technical leaders who need to scale high-performance teams.



\*\*Q: Can I use Coding5s to train my team of 50 developers?\*\*

A: Yes. Coding5s is under the MIT license, which allows for enterprise use without limits or licensing costs. All your team needs is access to Excel and the Creator Kit or Student Kit files. The internal implementation (if you want to integrate it with your systems, SSO, or internal platforms) is up to your development team, but the framework itself is free to use and modify.



\*\*Q: How do I measure the ROI of implementing Coding5s in my company?\*\*

A: You measure three key metrics: (1) Reduction in onboarding time for new developers, (2) Decrease in production bugs (because developers learn to audit code, not just write it), and (3) Improvement in the team's capacity to maintain legacy code (which makes up 80% of real work).



\*\*Q: Does this replace the internal bootcamps we already pay for?\*\*

A: It does not replace them; it complements them. Traditional bootcamps teach specific stacks (React, Django, etc.). Coding5s teaches meta-skills: how to learn any stack quickly, how to audit AI-generated code, and how to maintain complex systems. It is the "learning how to learn" layer that is missing from most training programs.



\*\*Q: Can I integrate this with our specific tech stack (React, Django, AWS)?\*\*

A: Absolutely. Coding5s is stack-agnostic. You can create specific courses for your stack using the Creator Kit. For cases where you need \*\*cumulative knowledge\*\* (lessons that build upon previous ones), the third pillar, \*\*Stateful5s\*\*, is the one that applies. In the repository, we include a baseline implementation of CCNA over Packet Tracer as a demonstration. If your case requires custom cumulative content, it would be necessary to work on that specific implementation.



\*\*Q: How fast can my team start using this?\*\*

A: In a matter of hours. In the repository, we are including pre-built Creator Kits and Student Kits for \*\*Python\*\* and \*\*Elixir\*\* (both for Coding5s and Stateful5s). If your stack matches those languages, your team can start using it the very same day. If you need a different stack, a senior developer can generate the first course in just a few hours using the Creator Kit.

\## 🎓 For Bootcamps and Universities



Questions from institutions that need to integrate Coding5s into their programs.



\*\*Q: Can I integrate Coding5s into my current curriculum without rewriting everything?\*\*

A: Coding5s does not take your current exercises and magically convert them. What you can do is: (1) analyze the Creator Kits and Student Kits available in the repository and use them directly if they meet your expectations, or (2) create your own kits in a matter of hours using the Creator Kit. You decide the degree of integration with your existing curriculum.



\*\*Q: Does this replace my teachers or complement them?\*\*

A: It complements them. Coding5s does not replace the teacher; it frees them from repetitive tasks (correcting syntax, hunting down basic errors) so they can focus on what only a human can do: architecture mentorship, code design reviews, and career guidance. The teacher becomes a "learning architect" instead of a "code corrector."



\*\*Q: How do I handle 200 simultaneous students with this system?\*\*

A: Coding5s is built for massive scale. As a teacher, you only need to explain once how to use the Student Kits and give general guidelines. You can handle 200 or 10,000 students with the same effort it previously took to manage 30. With a single email enclosing the Student Kit and the proper instructions, everyone will know what to do.



\*\*Q: Can I white-label this (put my logo on it)?\*\*

A: Yes. Coding5s is under the MIT license, meaning you can modify it, adapt it, and distribute it under your own brand. You only need to keep the original copyright notice in the source code.



\*\*Q: Is there technical support or consulting available for implementation?\*\*

A: Coding5s is in its early stages and is just beginning to build a community. What we are looking for is to invite institutions and educators to collaborate: report bugs, suggest changes, and improve the method together. If your institution wants to be an early adopter and help shape the framework, open an "Issue" or "Discussion" on GitHub.

\## 🔬 For Researchers and Academics



Questions from the academic community looking to validate and extend the methodology.



\*\*Q: Are there academic papers supporting this methodology?\*\*

A: Coding5s aligns with recent research on technical education in the AI era. Specifically, it implements principles discussed in papers such as \*"AI-Augmented Learning: Navigating the Epistemic Debt in Software Engineering Education"\* (https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.20206), which addresses the epistemic debt generated by the indiscriminate use of AIs. The framework operates under the concepts of "Controlled Cognitive Friction" and a "Consultant Stance" (treating the AI as a consultant, not a contractor), aligning with principles of the SOLO Taxonomy and the "Brownfield Triage" approach (auditing existing code).



\*\*Q: How can I use Coding5s for my technical education research?\*\*

A: Coding5s is completely open source and extensible. You can use the framework as a baseline for your research, adapt the Seed Contexts for your own experiments, and publish your results. We ask that you cite the repository and share your findings with the community.



\*\*Q: Can I cite the framework in my publications?\*\*

A: Yes. Coding5s is under the MIT license and can be cited in academic publications. Please use the following citation:

> Wilfredo Barrios (2026). \*Coding5s: A Framework for Controlled Cognitive Friction in Post-AI Technical Education\*. GitHub: github.com/WilfredoBarrios/coding5s-framework



\*\*Q: Is there empirical data on the effectiveness of the method?\*\*

A: We are in the process of gathering empirical data from early implementations. The first use cases (including the Stateful5s CCNA course with 69 lessons of accumulative context) show qualitative promise, but rigorous quantitative data will be published as implementations scale up. We invite academic institutions to collaborate on formal studies.



\*\*Q: How does it align with established pedagogical theories (Constructivism, Bloom, SOLO)?\*\*

A: Coding5s integrates multiple pedagogical frameworks: (1) \*\*Constructivism\*\*: the student builds their own knowledge through controlled friction, (2) \*\*Bloom's Taxonomy\*\*: the 5 stages progress from "Remembering" to "Creating", (3) \*\*SOLO Taxonomy\*\*: the Explanation Gates evaluate whether the student reached the "Relational" or "Extended Abstract" level, (4) \*\*Problem-Based Learning\*\*: each stage presents a real-world problem that the student must solve.
