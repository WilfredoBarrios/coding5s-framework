## 🏢 1. Introduction: The General Relativity of Context

*   **The Problem:** **Latent Drift** (the tendency of LLMs to regress to generic statistical means) and **Cognitive Outsourcing** (students offloading critical thinking to an unconstrained AI).
*   **The Solution:** The Seed Context functions as a **Cognitive Micro-controller of Gravity**, injecting immutable constraints that prevent *Epistemic Debt* by forcing the AI to orbit around strict pedagogical and architectural parameters.
*   **The Analogy:** If the *Language Seed Context* is Special Relativity (A localized solution for syntactic and linguistic output calibration), the *General Seed Context* is General Relativity—the universal law governing the curvature of the LLM's latent space.

## 🏗️ 2. The Three-Level Architecture (The ECP Engine)

To generate a final Seed Context, we utilize a tiered production pipeline:

### The Workflow: How to generate a final Seed
1.  **Level 0 (The Foundry):** Use the *Universal Seed Context Foundry* (provided below in Section 3) to generate a specialized Meta-Prompt.
2.  **Level 1 (The Factory):** Take the output of Level 0 and input your specific dimension parameters (e.g., Tech Stack, Friction Level) to create the *Seed Factory*.
3.  **Level 2 (The Payload):** Run the Level 1 Factory to generate the final, dense 150-250 word paragraph to be injected into the `[SCL_STREAM]`.
