# 🧪 Coding5s Research Lab

This is the incubator for **disruptive architectural patterns** and experimental protocols within the Coding5s framework. The ideas housed here are currently in active R&D; they represent the frontier of how we manage context, state, and interaction with Large Language Models.

---

### 📂 Lab Modules

*   **`EPHEMERAL_CONTEXT_PROTOCOL.md`**: Research on a compressed, Machine-to-Machine (M2M) markup language. It minimizes token overhead by relying on latent semantic inference, enabling "headless" AI workflows that maintain state across ephemeral sessions.
*   **`GENERAL_SEED_CONTEXT.md`**: Documentation of the "Cognitive Gravity" architecture. This pattern uses tiered meta-prompts to inject immutable pedagogical constraints, preventing latent drift and epistemic debt in AI mentors.
*   **`/reverse_pitch_marketing_technique`**: An unconventional B2B outreach protocol that replaces biased sales narratives with "evidence-bounded" audit payloads, transforming the buyer's own LLM into a neutral technical auditor.

---

### 🔬 Research Philosophy

The modules in this folder are **highly experimental**. They are designed to solve fundamental limitations of current AI infrastructure (token limits, latency, model drift). 

**Lab Mission:**
1. **Bridge to Production:** Successful experiments are promoted to core pillars of the Coding5s framework.
2. **Document & Iterate:** Every hypothesis is documented, even if it hasn't been proven yet.
3. **Challenge Convention:** We assume that current prompt engineering standards are suboptimal; we aim to build the next-generation infrastructure.

*Note: These protocols are the "engine room" of the project. If you are exploring these modules, you are looking at the foundational logic that powers our framework.*
